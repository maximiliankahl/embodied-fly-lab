How to read this replay

  • Recorded, not live. Brain, bridge, body and verifier ran offline in Python (flylab); this page only plays back the exported files. Each run lists the command that reproduces it.
  • Brain colours show the mean firing rate of each neuron over a 1 s simulation of the whole-brain leaky integrate-and-fire model (Shiu et al. 2024). It is not time-resolved and runs open loop: the body does not feed back into the brain.
  • What the connectome decides: which descending neurons become active. The ventral nerve cord is not simulated; a frozen, hand-designed bridge turns descending-neuron rates into a 3-number walking drive (or a flight command). Leg and wing movements come from the body's physics controller, not from neurons. A body that can walk or fly is not by itself evidence of connectome control.
  • Verifier verdicts are recomputed from the raw body trajectory, independently of the body's own behaviour label, and compared with the published experiment cited on each run.
  • Agent hypotheses come from the live Omnigent run record and are labelled as agent-generated; none is confirmed in the wet lab.
